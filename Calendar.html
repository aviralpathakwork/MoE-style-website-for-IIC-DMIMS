01 / Annual Overview
The Year Ahead

From Ideas to Impact.

The Institution's Innovation Council at DMIMS aims to build a vibrant innovation ecosystem where students discover opportunities, solve real-world problems, develop business ideas and connect with entrepreneurs, mentors and industry.

The annual calendar follows a structured journey from inspiration and ideation to validation, prototyping, fundraising and startup development.

OUR MISSION
Think. Build. Connect.

Empowering students to transform ideas into meaningful initiatives, ventures and solutions.

The Innovation Pipeline
Q1 / SEP–NOV 01. INSPIRE

Discover problems and generate ideas.

Q2 / DEC–FEB 02. VALIDATE

Test concepts and develop business models.

Q3 / MAR–MAY 03. BUILD

Develop prototypes and business plans.

Q4 / JUN–AUG 04. SCALE

Pitch, connect and launch ventures.

OUR ANNUAL JOURNEY

IDEA → INNOVATION → IMPACT

One ecosystem. Four quarters. A thousand possibilities.

07 / Performance Framework
Measuring What Matters

From Activity to Impact.

Proposed institutional targets for academic year 2026–27.

500+Cumulative student participations
50+Innovation ideas generated
15+Shortlisted teams
20+Business Model Canvases
10+Prototypes / MVPs
3+Student startups supported

Activity Level Classification

  • Level 1: 2–4 hours – Expert talks, mentoring.
  • Level 2: 5–8 hours – Workshops, seminars.
  • Level 3: 9–18 hours – Bootcamps, competitions.
  • Level 4: More than 18 hours – Extended hackathons, tech fests.

Documentation & Compliance

  • Approved activity proposals.
  • Posters, invitations and resource person details.
  • Attendance and photographs.
  • Feedback and measurable outcomes.
  • Reports, certificates and evidence.
  • IIC portal uploads and quarterly reviews.

Suggested Ecosystem Partners

  • Nagpur and Vidarbha entrepreneurs.
  • MSMEs and industry professionals.
  • Incubation centres and innovation labs.
  • DMIMS alumni and startup founders.
  • IPR experts and investors.

Monitoring & Scoring

  • Quarterly weightage: 0.25 each.
  • Offline: full assigned activity weightage.
  • Online: 75% of assigned weightage.
  • Calendar contributes 40% of Activity Score.
  • Activity Score contributes 80% of Star Rating.

Targets are proposed planning targets. Scoring information is based on the supplied IIC 9.0 framework and should be checked against current official guidelines.
