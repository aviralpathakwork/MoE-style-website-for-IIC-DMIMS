Part A

Common IIC Challenge

Every applicant must complete this part regardless of their selected role.

🚀 Imagine This

IIC DMIMS wants to increase student participation in innovation, entrepreneurship and startup activities.

Design one activity that IIC can conduct within the next 30 days.

Include These Points

1. Activity name
2. Problem / objective
3. Target students
4. How the activity will work
5. Resources required
6. Expected outcome
7. How you will promote it

Important: No AI Use

This assessment is designed to understand your own thinking, creativity and initiative. Therefore, your submitted document must be your original work.

✓ Do the assignment yourself.
✓ Do not use ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot or other AI tools to generate your submission.
✓ You may use normal internet research for understanding a topic, but the final work must be written in your own words.
✓ Do not copy another applicant's work.
✓ We value genuine thinking more than perfect English or fancy design.
Submission Format

What Should Your Document Look Like?

Keep it simple. We are evaluating your ideas, not the number of pages.

📄 Recommended Format

  • PDF format
  • Maximum 3–5 pages
  • Clearly mention your name
  • Mention your preferred role
  • Use clear headings
  • Keep the information structured
  • Use your own words

✨ What We Look For

  • Original thinking
  • Practical ideas
  • Problem-solving ability
  • Initiative
  • Clarity of thought
  • Understanding of your selected role
  • Willingness to take ownership
Evaluation

How Your Assignment Will Be Evaluated

There is no need to make your document unnecessarily complicated. Strong thinking matters more than decoration.

Parameter What We Check Marks
Role Understanding Do you understand what your selected role involves? 10
Problem Solving Can you identify a problem and suggest a practical solution? 10
Creativity Is your approach thoughtful and different? 5
Initiative Have you gone beyond basic instructions? 5
Clarity Can you explain your idea clearly? 5
Practicality Can your idea realistically be implemented at DMIMS? 5
Total Maximum Score 40
