Start-up Coordinator Assessment

01 • Role Understanding

What Does a Start-up Coordinator Do?

My understanding is that the Start-up Coordinator should not only organise startup events. The role should help identify students who have ideas and create a path for those ideas to move forward.

🔎 Identify

Find students who have business ideas, startup interests or solutions to real problems.

🤝 Connect

Connect students with mentors, founders, incubators, competitions and useful resources.

🎤 Enable

Create pitching opportunities where students can explain and test their ideas.

📈 Track

Maintain a simple database of ideas, founders, activities and progress.

My Approach

If I am selected, I would focus on creating a system where a student does not feel that their idea ends after one event. The objective would be to create a journey from an idea to validation, mentorship and possible execution.

IDEA Student thought
VALIDATE Understand problem
CONNECT Mentor / ecosystem
ACTION Prototype / pitch

Why This Role?

I am interested in entrepreneurship and I like the process of taking an idea and converting it into something practical. I also think students may have good ideas but may not know whom to approach or what their next step should be.

02 • Common Challenge

IDEA TO IMPACT — DMIMS

IIC wants to increase student participation in innovation and entrepreneurship. My proposed activity is designed to make students participate without requiring them to already have a complete startup.

🚀 Activity: IDEA TO IMPACT

A one-week student challenge where participants identify a real problem, develop a simple solution, validate it with other students and present their idea at a final pitch session.

Problem

Many students may have ideas but do not know how to start. Some may also believe that they need a complete business plan before approaching an entrepreneurship initiative.

Objective

  • Encourage students to identify real problems.
  • Introduce students to basic startup thinking.
  • Give students an opportunity to present ideas.
  • Identify promising student innovators.
  • Connect selected ideas with mentors.

Target Students

The activity can be open to all DMIMS students, especially students interested in startups, innovation, business, technology and social impact.

Expected Output

Student Ideas Collection of practical problems and possible solutions.
Shortlisted Ideas Promising ideas selected for further support.
Student Pitching Students get practical presentation exposure.
Mentorship Selected teams can be connected to mentors.

Execution Plan

03 • Execution

How I Would Execute It

I would keep the activity simple enough for students to participate while still creating useful outputs for IIC.

DAY 1
Problem Hunt

Students identify one problem they observe in college, society, business or daily life.

DAY 2
Idea Development

Students explain who faces the problem, why it matters and what solution they propose.

DAY 3
Validation

Students speak to potential users and collect basic feedback about their idea.

DAY 4
Mentor Interaction

IIC can arrange a short interaction with an entrepreneur, faculty member or mentor.

DAY 5
Pitch Preparation

Students prepare a short presentation covering problem, solution, target user and next step.

DAY 7
Idea Pitch Day

Shortlisted students present their ideas to a small panel and receive feedback.

Resources Required

Resource Purpose
Classroom / Seminar Hall Mentor session and final pitch.
Google Form Idea registration and participant details.
Google Sheet Track ideas, participants and progress.
Mentor / Entrepreneur Give practical feedback.
IIC Team Coordination, promotion and documentation.

Promotion & Measurement

04 • Promotion & Results

How I Would Promote the Activity

The promotion should make students feel that they are being invited to solve a problem, not simply attend another college event.

📱 WhatsApp

Short message with a clear challenge, deadline and registration link.

📸 Instagram

Poster + short reel explaining the challenge in student-friendly language.

🎤 Classroom Promotion

Personally explain the challenge in classes and encourage students with ideas to apply.

👥 Student Network

Ask IIC volunteers and class representatives to reach students directly.

Example Promotion Message

Have you ever noticed a problem and thought, "Someone should solve this"?

Now try to solve it.

Join IDEA TO IMPACT by IIC DMIMS and turn one real-world problem into a practical idea.

No startup experience required. Just bring a problem worth solving.

How I Would Measure Success

Metric What I Would Track
Registrations Number of students who register.
Participation Number of students who actually complete the challenge.
Ideas Generated Total number of ideas submitted.
Shortlisted Ideas Ideas that show potential for further development.
Mentor Connections Number of students connected with mentors.
Follow-up Number of ideas that continue after the activity.
