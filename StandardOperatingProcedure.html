Leadership

IIC Convenor — Overall Management

The Convenor acts as the central point of coordination for the Core Team and ensures that every vertical works towards the same IIC objectives.

👑 Management Responsibilities

  • Supervise all IIC Core Team activities.
  • Assign responsibilities and coordinate verticals.
  • Review proposed activities and initiatives.
  • Coordinate with relevant faculty/institutional authorities.
  • Set priorities and activity timelines.
  • Monitor progress of every coordinator.

📊 Review & Accountability

  • Conduct regular Core Team meetings.
  • Review weekly/monthly progress.
  • Identify delays or inactive responsibilities.
  • Resolve coordination issues between teams.
  • Review event reports and documentation.
  • Maintain overall accountability of the council.

Operating Rhythm

Weekly & Monthly Responsibilities

The team should maintain a predictable working rhythm instead of becoming active only before events.

WEEKLY

Core Team Review

  • Review current activities.
  • Update progress of each vertical.
  • Discuss upcoming events.
  • Identify pending responsibilities.
  • Share opportunities and student ideas.
  • Escalate problems to the Convenor.
MONTHLY

IIC Performance Review

  • Review activities completed.
  • Review participation numbers.
  • Review startup/innovation outcomes.
  • Review social media performance.
  • Review documentation status.
  • Plan next month's initiatives.

Documentation SOP

📝 Event Report

Purpose, date, venue, speakers, activities, participation and outcomes.

👥 Attendance

Maintain participant and volunteer attendance where applicable.

🏆 Achievements

Record student startups, competitions, awards, recognitions and outcomes.

📁 Google Drive

Keep reports, certificates, photographs and supporting evidence organised.

📊 Data

Maintain structured records so information can be retrieved when required.

Core Team Guidelines

How an IIC member is expected to work.

01

Take Ownership

If you accept a responsibility, follow it through until completion.

02

Communicate

Keep the Convenor and relevant team members updated.

03

Meet Deadlines

Respect timelines, especially before events and submissions.

04

Work as One Team

Coordinators should collaborate rather than work in isolated groups.

05

Be Professional

Maintain proper communication and behaviour with students, faculty, guests and external stakeholders.

06

Document Your Work

Activities should be properly recorded and evidence should not be left until the end of the year.

07

Ask When Stuck

Problems should be communicated early instead of remaining hidden.

08

Represent IIC Responsibly

Every Core Team member represents IIC DMIMS when interacting with students and external stakeholders.

Don't just hold a designation. Own a responsibility.
