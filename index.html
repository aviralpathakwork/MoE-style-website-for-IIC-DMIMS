ABOUT IIC

Building an Innovation Culture

The Institution's Innovation Council provides a platform for students to explore innovation, entrepreneurship, intellectual property, startups and industry interaction.

At DMIMS, IIC aims to create opportunities where students can move from ideas to action.

01

Think Different

Question problems, create ideas and find better solutions.

IIC Activities

Student startup ideas, pitching, mentoring and entrepreneurship initiatives.

STUDENT COUNCIL

Council Roles

Explore the responsibilities of the IIC Student Council.

01

Start-up Coordinator

Scout student business ideas and facilitate pitching, mentoring and incubation support.

02

Internship Coordinator

Facilitate startup and industry internship opportunities for students.

03

IPR Coordinator

Create awareness about patents, copyrights, trademarks and intellectual property.

04

Graphics & Creative

Design posters, banners, brochures, presentations and promotional creatives.

05

YUKTI Coordinator

Identify innovative student projects and coordinate YUKTI submissions.

06

WISE Student Lead

Promote women innovation, entrepreneurship and community outreach.

07

ARIIA Coordinator

Organize innovation metrics, achievements and institutional data.

08

Alumni Coordinator

Connect entrepreneur alumni and experts for mentoring and collaboration.

09

Social Media Coordinator

Manage social media, event coverage, captions and digital outreach.

10

Documentation & Logistics

Manage event logistics, meeting minutes, documentation and reports.

IMPORTANT

IIC requires commitment.

Selected Core Committee members are expected to dedicate themselves to IIC and cannot simultaneously be part of another college club or committee during their IIC tenure.
