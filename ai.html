WHAT THE JUDGES EXPECT

Show your working solution

A good automation project solves a clearly defined problem and demonstrates measurable usefulness.

🎯

1. Identify the Problem

Explain the real-world problem your project solves, who faces it, and why automation is useful.

🔗

2. Explain the Workflow

Show the trigger, input data, processing steps, integrations, conditions, and final output.

🖥️

3. Run a Live Demo

Execute your workflow with sample data and demonstrate that the expected output is actually produced.

🤖

4. Explain the AI

If AI is used, explain what it does, what information it receives, and how you check its output.

📈

5. Show the Value

Describe time saved, manual effort reduced, errors prevented, or improvements for the target user.

🛡️

6. Know the Limitations

Discuss failure cases, privacy, reliability, costs, and how you would improve the project in the future.

YOUR PRESENTATION FLOW

How to demonstrate your project

Use this simple sequence to make your demonstration clear, structured, and easy to understand.

01

Introduce the problem

Briefly explain the problem, the intended user, and what happens when the problem is not solved.

02

Show the workflow architecture

Walk through your nodes, triggers, APIs or integrations, decision branches, and output.

03

Execute a real test

Use prepared sample data and run the automation. Show the resulting message, report, record, or other output.

04

Explain the impact

Describe how the solution saves effort or improves accuracy. Use actual measurements where available.

05

Answer questions confidently

Be prepared to discuss technical choices, errors, security, limitations, and future improvements.
