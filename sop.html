Main Job: Build the startup ecosystem among DMIMS students.

Key Responsibilities

  • Find students with startup or business ideas.
  • Maintain a database of student entrepreneurs.
  • Connect promising ideas with mentors and incubators.
  • Organise startup pitching sessions.
  • Help students prepare basic pitch decks.
  • Conduct startup and entrepreneurship workshops.
  • Identify potential student startups for IIC activities.
  • Encourage participation in startup competitions and hackathons.
⭐ Best for: Students interested in startups, business, entrepreneurship and networking.

Main Job: Create internship and practical-exposure opportunities for students.

Key Responsibilities

  • Find startups, companies and organisations offering internships.
  • Build relationships with founders and HR professionals.
  • Share relevant internship opportunities with students.
  • Maintain an internship opportunity database.
  • Coordinate startup and company interaction sessions.
  • Help organise internship fairs and networking events.
  • Track student participation.
  • Track internship outcomes and opportunities.
⭐ Best for: Students good at communication, networking and professional coordination.

IPR = Intellectual Property Rights. Create awareness about patents, copyrights, trademarks, designs and intellectual property.

Key Responsibilities

  • Organise IPR awareness sessions.
  • Explain basic patent, copyright and trademark concepts.
  • Identify student innovations with potential IP value.
  • Connect innovators with appropriate experts.
  • Promote patent and IPR initiatives.
  • Maintain records of IPR-related activities.
  • Create simple educational content on IPR.
Example: A student develops a unique product or business idea. The IPR Coordinator helps them understand: "Can this idea or creation be protected?"
⭐ Best for: Students interested in law, innovation, research and intellectual property.

Main Job: Support IIC-related innovation documentation and institutional innovation activities.

HOW IT WORKS

From Idea to Impact

IIC works as one team. Different coordinators own different verticals, but the final objective is shared.

Identify

Find ideas, students, opportunities, problems and innovation potential.

Connect

Bring together students, mentors, founders, alumni and experts.

Execute

Convert ideas into workshops, competitions, events and initiatives.

Document

Record activities, achievements, participation and measurable outcomes.
