The Start-up Coordinator identifies students with business ideas and helps connect them with the right ecosystem, mentors, competitions and opportunities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Find students with startup or business ideas.
  • Maintain a database of student entrepreneurs.
  • Connect ideas with mentors and incubators.
  • Identify startup competitions and opportunities.
  • Organise startup pitching sessions.
  • Help students prepare basic pitch decks.
  • Conduct entrepreneurship workshops.
  • Identify potential student startups for IIC.
  • Encourage participation in hackathons and competitions.

Best For

Students interested in startups, business, entrepreneurship and networking.

Startups Business Entrepreneurship Networking

The Internship Coordinator builds relationships with startups, companies and organisations to bring meaningful internship opportunities to students.

Key Responsibilities

  • Find companies offering internships.
  • Build relationships with founders and HR professionals.
  • Share relevant internship opportunities with students.
  • Maintain an internship opportunity database.
  • Coordinate company-student interactions.
  • Support internship fairs and networking events.
  • Track student participation and outcomes.

Best For

Students who enjoy communication, networking, professional coordination and relationship building.

IPR stands for Intellectual Property Rights. This role helps students understand patents, copyrights, trademarks and designs related to their innovative ideas.

Key Responsibilities

  • Organise IPR awareness sessions.
  • Explain basic patent, copyright and trademark concepts.
  • Identify student innovations with potential IP value.
  • Connect students with relevant experts.
  • Promote IP-related initiatives and competitions.
  • Prepare educational content on IPR.
  • Maintain records of IPR-related activities.

Example

A student develops a unique product or business concept. The IPR Coordinator helps the student understand: “Can this idea or creation be protected?”

How IIC Works

From an idea to impact.

The Core Team works together across different roles to turn student ideas into meaningful initiatives.

STEP 01

Identify

Discover student ideas, innovators, startups, opportunities and problems worth solving.

STEP 02

Connect

Connect students with mentors, alumni, founders, competitions and relevant opportunities.

STEP 03

Execute

Organise workshops, events, challenges, competitions and innovation activities.

STEP 04

Document

Capture outcomes, participation, achievements, reports and evidence for future impact.
